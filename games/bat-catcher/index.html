Bat Catcher

Score0

Move the net with your finger, the mouse or the arrow keys, and tap or press Space to swing. A bat is 10 points. Gold bats are worth 30. Back-to-back catches build a multiplier, and a miss resets it. Leave the crows alone: a hit costs 20. A cackling witch sometimes flies past. She can't be caught, and swinging at her only counts as a miss. You have forty seconds before dawn.
